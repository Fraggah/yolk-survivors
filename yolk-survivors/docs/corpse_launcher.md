# Corpse Launcher: landed egg explosive

All four tiers lob the approved fried egg sprite toward a snapshot of the selected enemy's position. The egg stays horizontally aligned independently of aim direction. Its visual arc uses `lob_height`, with flight time determined by projectile_speed and distance. A swept footprint detects the first targetable enemy hurtbox on the route and applies a small knockback without damage, statuses, critical rolls or lifesteal. On contact it stops traveling, falls to that floor position over `egg_fall_duration` (0.1 s), then starts its fuse. If it misses all enemies, it lands at the original target snapshot. The original damaging hitbox remains disabled throughout travel/rest.

On landing it rests on the surface, pulses warm, then explodes after `fuse` (default 0.3 s). The one-shot oval blast uses the normal ranged effective damage, critical, on-hit status, lifesteal and damage recording path. Default radius is 80 before entity scale and the player's AoE bonus, with Y ratio 0.6. Camera visibility does not cancel a placed egg. Pause stops flight and fuse; wave cleanup removes pending eggs without detonating them.

Each explosion creates one normal collectible yolk of value 1 at the landing position, even if it hits no enemies. This is independent of normal enemy loot. The egg has a detonation guard, so it cannot pay twice. Bonus yolks share normal pickup, unused-yolk reserve and run cleanup behavior through Arena.spawn_yolk_at.
